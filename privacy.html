Privacy Policy — batzoum_tiktok

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data used

The app uses TikTok's authorization to upload videos to the authorizing user's drafts. It receives an access token and a refresh token for that user's account.

2. Storage

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party, and no TikTok user data is collected beyond what is needed to upload videos.

3. Revoking access

The user can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok settings, and can delete the stored tokens from their computer.
